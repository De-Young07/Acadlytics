# Acadlytics — Assumptions & Limitations

## 1. Data Assumptions

- GPA scale is consistent across years  
- Course grading standards are comparable  
- Credit units reflect course importance  
- Student IDs are consistent across datasets  

---

## 2. Statistical Assumptions

- Observations are independent  
- Performance metrics are comparable across cohorts  
- Score distributions are stable across sessions  

---

## 3. Data Limitations

- No lecturer-course linkage  
- No graduation data  
- No alumni outcomes  
- Limited sample data for some datasets  

---

## 4. Implications

- Lecturer effectiveness cannot be fully evaluated  
- Graduation analysis is not yet possible  
- Long-term impact analysis is limited
